## What Is FreeAgent and How Does It Work for Builders?

FreeAgent is cloud-based accounting software designed to help small businesses, sole traders and self-employed people manage their financial records.

Instead of keeping everything on paper or trying to work out your income and expenses at the end of the tax year, FreeAgent helps you organise your bookkeeping throughout the year.

For a self-employed builder or tradesperson, FreeAgent can help you:

- Record payments received from customers and contractors.
- Keep track of business expenses, including tools, materials and fuel.
- Connect a business bank account to import transactions.
- Create and manage invoices.
- Record relevant Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) deductions.
- Keep digital records for Making Tax Digital.
- Use supported features to submit information to HMRC.

This can make it easier to understand how your business is performing and reduce the amount of paperwork you have to deal with.

## Do You Need a Separate Business Bank Account for FreeAgent?

Having a dedicated business bank account can make bookkeeping much easier.

Although sole traders are not generally required to have a separate business bank account, keeping business and personal spending apart helps you see exactly what’s happening with your business finances.

For example, imagine you’re a self-employed carpenter.

During one week you might receive:

- £1,200 from a building contractor.
- £450 for a private carpentry job.
- £300 for a small repair job.

You might also pay for timber, screws, fuel, tools and business insurance.

If your business bank account is connected to FreeAgent, these transactions can appear automatically in the software, ready to be checked and categorised.

That saves time compared with entering every payment manually.

However, the transactions still need to be checked carefully. A bank feed doesn’t automatically know whether every payment is business-related or how it should be treated for tax purposes.

## Is FreeAgent Suitable for CIS Subcontractors?

Yes. FreeAgent includes functionality to help record Construction Industry Scheme deductions.

This is particularly useful for self-employed builders, carpenters, plumbers, electricians and other subcontractors who work for construction contractors.

Under CIS, contractors may deduct tax from payments before paying the subcontractor.

For example:

- Gross labour payment: **£1,000**
- CIS tax deducted: **£200**
- Amount received in your bank: **£800**

Your bookkeeping needs to reflect the full £1,000 gross income and the £200 CIS deduction separately.

Simply recording the £800 bank receipt as your total earnings would not give the complete picture.

CIS deductions are taken into account when calculating your final tax liability.

## Will Builders Need FreeAgent for Making Tax Digital in 2027?

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is being introduced gradually.

The qualifying income thresholds are:

- **6 April 2026:** Qualifying income over £50,000.
- **6 April 2027:** Qualifying income over £30,000.
- **6 April 2028:** Qualifying income over £20,000.

Qualifying income generally means your total gross income from self-employment and property, before deducting business expenses or tax.

For example, a self-employed builder with £40,000 in qualifying income for the 2025/26 tax year would generally fall within Making Tax Digital from April 2027, unless an exemption or other exception applies.

Builders who fall within the rules will need to maintain digital records and send quarterly updates using compatible software.

## Can FreeAgent Submit MTD Quarterly Updates to HMRC?

FreeAgent offers Making Tax Digital functionality, but you must use the appropriate features for your tax obligations.

Under Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, eligible self-employed people must maintain digital records and send quarterly income and expense updates to HMRC.

For those using standard tax-year quarters, the usual deadlines are:

- **7 August** — first quarterly update.
- **7 November** — second quarterly update.
- **7 February** — third quarterly update.
- **7 May** — fourth quarterly update.

These updates are not the same as completing your annual tax return.

You still need to deal with your end-of-year tax obligations and any required adjustments.

The important thing is to keep your records up to date throughout the year rather than leave everything until a deadline approaches.

## Is FreeAgent Difficult for Builders to Use?

FreeAgent is designed to make bookkeeping more manageable, but there is still a learning curve if you’ve never used accounting software before.

You’ll need to understand how to record different types of income and expenses, deal with CIS deductions where relevant and check that your bookkeeping records are accurate.

For example, buying materials for a customer’s job may need to be treated differently from buying an item for personal use.

You may also need to distinguish between everyday business expenses and purchases of equipment that require different tax treatment.

If you’ve spent years working on building sites rather than in an office, you might understandably prefer to leave the bookkeeping to somebody else.
